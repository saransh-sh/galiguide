Artisan Oral Heritage

“A good shoe doesn’t belong in a landfill just because the thread surrendered. Give me 10 minutes and genuine leather will outlive the monsoon.”

Ramesh Ji inherited this station under the Peepal tree from his father in 1996. He has witnessed Sarafa transition from pure bullion goldsmith shops by day into India’s celebrated midnight gastronomic hub by night. His toolkit includes a cast-iron shoemaker’s last stamped 1942.
